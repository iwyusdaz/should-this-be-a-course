# Attribution

## Case and materials

- **Organization, people, interviews, survey data, HRIS metrics:** *Brightline Health Services* and everyone in it are **fictional**. All interview notes, survey responses and baseline metrics in this repository were **constructed for portfolio purposes** (generated and hand-authored by the designer; see [survey data README](https://iwyusdaz.github.io/should-this-be-a-course/02-discovery/survey-data-readme.html) for how the CSV was produced). They do not describe any real organization or real people. Any resemblance to real persons or organizations is coincidental.

## Frameworks and models referenced (ideas, not copied text)

- **Gilbert's Behavior Engineering Model** — Thomas F. Gilbert, *Human Competence: Engineering Worthy Performance* (ISPI, 1978/2007). Used as an analytic framework; the application to the fictional case is original.
- **Mager & Pipe performance analysis flow** — Robert F. Mager & Peter Pipe, *Analyzing Performance Problems* (CEP Press). Redrawn as a Mermaid diagram for this case; structure follows the published model, wording paraphrased.
- **Action Mapping** — Cathy Moore, https://blog.cathy-moore.com/action-mapping-a-visual-approach-to-training-design/ . Used as a design method; the map itself is original to this case.
- **WCAG 2.1** — W3C, https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/ . Accessibility requirements referenced, not reproduced in full.
