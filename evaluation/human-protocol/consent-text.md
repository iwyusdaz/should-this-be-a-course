# Consent text (usability sessions)

Read aloud (or send) before the session begins. The participant keeps a copy. No session starts without verbal (or written) agreement to this text.

---

**Thank you for helping test a draft training scenario.**

**What this is.** You will play through a short fictional training scenario and tell me what you think. The scenario and the organization in it ("Brightline Health Services") are fictional; nothing you see describes real people.

**What will happen.** About 45 minutes: you play the scenario once, then again while thinking aloud, then answer a few questions. With your permission, I will record the screen and audio so I can note what happened without interrupting you.

**Your data.**

- I will use a first name or a pseudonym you choose. I do not need your employer, your workplace, or any identifying details.
- Recordings are used only to write up findings, are stored on a password-protected device, and are **deleted within 30 days** after the write-up is done.
- Quotes: I may quote things you say in the findings — attributed only to "participant" with a number (e.g., P2), never to you by name.
- You can ask me to delete your recording at any time, during or after the session, and I will.
- I am not testing *you*. Anything confusing or broken is a finding about the draft, not about you.

**Your choices during the session.** You can skip any question, pause, or stop entirely at any point, for any reason, without giving a reason.

**What happens to the findings.** Findings are used to improve the draft and may appear in a public portfolio write-up, in the anonymous form described above.

**Questions?** Ask anything now or at any point. Agreeing to start the session counts as your consent to what is described above; you may withdraw that consent at any time.

---

*Note for the facilitator (not read aloud): do not record consent itself unless the participant explicitly agrees; a dated note that this text was read and agreed to is sufficient. Collect no data you do not need. Do not involve minors.*
