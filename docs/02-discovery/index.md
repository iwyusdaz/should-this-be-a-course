Discovery for the Brightline case: an interview guide, simulated interview notes, a simulated pulse survey (with its generation method fully documented), an analysis of the survey data, and a review of the organization's documents.

> **Simulated evidence.** The interviews, survey data, and documents in this section are **fictional and were constructed for portfolio purposes**. Brightline Health Services does not exist. The data was designed to be realistic (including noise and contradictions) and to demonstrate the discovery step of a performance consulting engagement.
