# Solution blueprint

> **Simulated case.** Brightline Health Services and all names and figures are fictional — constructed for portfolio purposes.

Every component of the blend, with its owner, timeline and dependencies. Weeks are counted from proposal approval (week 0 = approval; the requested deadline was "live next quarter", ~11 weeks).

| # | Component | Owner | Builds | Launches | Depends on | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One-on-one cadence standard + template** | Ops directors (HR supports) | Weeks 1–2 | Week 3 (with week-5 manager briefing) | COO approval of rostered time | Standard published; template in every manager's hands; time visible on rosters |
| 2 | **Review-form change** (2 qualitative questions) | HR | Weeks 1–3 | Next review cycle (and next quarter's form run) | HR policy sign-off | Form revised; old ratings-only version retired |
| 3 | **Span-of-control review** | Ops → COO | Weeks 2–5 | Week 6: options memo to COO | None (analysis already done) | COO receives the memo; decision logged |
| 4 | **"Feedback in 5 minutes" job aid** | L&D | Weeks 2–3 | Week 3 | Template (component 1) so the two share language | Printable + accessible HTML/PDF live; referenced by template |
| 5 | **Branching scenario** (15 min) | L&D | Weeks 2–6 (incl. testing) | Week 5 manager briefing | Job aid language (component 4) | Scenario live, tested for usability + accessibility |
| 6 | **30-day practice challenge** | L&D + HR | Weeks 4–6 | Week 5 (starts with the briefing) | Scenario + template | Challenge kit published; peer check-in pairs assigned |
| 7 | **Scorecard change** (team-development items) | COO | Weeks 3–8 | Next scorecard cycle | COO decision | Scorecards re-issued with team-development items |
| 8 | **Measurement setup** | L&D + HRIS | Weeks 4–6 | Week 5 (baseline locked) | Pulse survey instrument | Dashboards live; baseline validated against HRIS |

## Sequencing logic

- **Weeks 1–3 fix the environment** (cadence, template, form, job aid). Nothing about "training" launches before managers have somewhere to *do* the behavior.
- **Week 5 is the manager briefing** (30 minutes, not 2 hours): the standard, the template, the job aid, and the scenario — in that order — plus challenge sign-up.
- **The skill practice (scenario) and reinforcement (challenge) ride on top of the structure**, mirroring the [action map](action-map.html): no practice without a place to perform.
- **Measurement starts at launch**, not after: baseline was locked during discovery; the first post-launch pulse lands 6 weeks in.

## Component 1 detail: the one-on-one template (ships with the cadence standard)

> **Manager one-on-one — 15 minutes, every two weeks**
> *(Bring: this sheet + the "Feedback in 5 minutes" job aid)*
>
> **1. How are you? (2 min)** — one genuine open question; listen; note anything to follow up.
> **2. Wins and progress (4 min)** — "What went well since we last talked?" Name one specific thing you observed. *(Structure: situation → behavior → impact.)*
> **3. What's in the way? (4 min)** — "What's slowing you down?" If it's yours to fix, say when it will be fixed. If it isn't, say who owns it.
> **4. One piece of feedback (3 min)** — one specific, behavior-based piece of feedback (use the job aid). Ask for their view before agreeing next steps.
> **5. Next step + book the next one (2 min)** — agree one action each. Put the next one-on-one in the calendar *now*.
>
> *Notes: keep it their meeting, not a status update. Cancel only for clinical emergencies — reschedule within 48 hours.*

The template deliberately closes the loop (component A4 in the [action map](action-map.html)) and references the job aid so the structure travels together.
