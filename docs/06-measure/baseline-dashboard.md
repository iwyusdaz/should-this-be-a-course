# Baseline dashboard — where Brightline starts

> **Simulated data constructed for portfolio purposes.** Brightline Health Services is fictional. These are the baseline values the [measurement plan](measurement-plan.html) tracks against; the survey-derived figures come from the generated CSV documented in the [survey data README](../02-discovery/survey-data-readme.html). Post-launch quarters would appear here — none exist because there was no real deployment.

Managers also self-report that only about **1 in 5 of their one-on-ones (~20%)** contain structured feedback — so "more meetings" without structure would miss the point (the [Cedar Point finding](../02-discovery/survey-analysis.html)).

## 90-day turnover — trailing four quarters (HRIS, simulated)

| Quarter | New hires | Left within 90 days | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q4 2025 | 21 | 4 | 19.0% |
| Q1 2026 | 19 | 4 | 21.1% |
| Q2 2026 | 20 | 5 | 25.0% |
| Q3 2026 | 18 | 4 | 22.2% |

Exit-interview coding for the 17 exits: "no feedback / didn't feel supported" appears as a stated factor in **9 of 17** (HR, simulated coding). At an estimated $9,400 per new-hire exit, the trailing-year cost is ≈ **$160k**; reaching 15% avoids ≈ **$56k/year**.

## What happens to this page

After each quarterly pulse, the new numbers go on this dashboard next to the baseline, with the comparison-group split (wave 1 vs. wave 2 clinics) from the [measurement plan](measurement-plan.html). If the numbers stop matching the targets, the plan's stop/adjust rule applies — not a quieter chart.
